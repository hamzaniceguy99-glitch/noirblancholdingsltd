Our services, in detail

Four services for small and mid-sized businesses, each with a defined deliverable.

Program 01 · Where it actually goes

Operations review

We spend time where the work happens rather than only in meetings, because the gap between how a process is described and how it runs is usually where the problem lives. The output is a short written assessment with the two or three constraints that matter, ranked.

  • 2 to 4 weeks
  • Businesses
  • Fixed fee
  • Access to the work

Program 02 · Remove, then automate

Process & systems

Most businesses try to fix a bad process by buying software, which automates the mess at speed. We remove the unnecessary steps first, fix the handoffs where work sits waiting, and only then ask whether a system is needed — often it is not.

  • 3 to 6 weeks
  • Businesses
  • Fixed fee
  • Staff time

Program 03 · Contracts nobody has read

Cost & supplier review

Auto-renewing contracts are where money quietly leaves a business: software nobody uses, services sized for a company twice as large, terms agreed years ago. We list what is in force, what it costs and when notice is due, then work through it with you.

  • Businesses
  • Fixed fee
  • Contract access
