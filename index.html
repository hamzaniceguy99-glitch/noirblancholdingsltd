Frequently asked

The questions we get most

How is this different from a big consultancy?

Mostly that we stay for implementation and we scope narrowly. One problem, a fixed fee, and a short deliverable rather than a programme that grows.

Will you guarantee savings?

No. Anyone who does is guessing or building the guarantee into the fee. We will tell you what we found and what it is worth if implemented.

Can you advise on funding or investment?

No. That is financial and regulated work, we are not FCA authorised, and we will refer you.

Do you work on retainer?

We prefer not to. Open-ended retainers reward staying rather than finishing. Fixed scope and fee keeps the incentives honest.

Should we buy new software?

Often the honest answer is not yet. Automating a bad process makes it faster, not better. We fix the process first and then ask the question again.

How long before anything changes?

Process changes can show in weeks. Anything involving behaviour takes a quarter at least, and we will say so rather than promising a quick win.

Something not working?

A free first call on what is actually wrong, and whether we are the right help.
